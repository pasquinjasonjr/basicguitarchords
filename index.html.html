    2. Essential Tips for Beginners

    Keep these habits in mind during your daily 15-minute practice sessions.

    Press Right Behind the Fret

    Place your fingertips just behind the metal fret wire rather than in the middle of the space. This prevents buzzing strings with less effort.

    Curve Your Fingers

    Keep your knuckles arched so the fleshy pads of your fingers do not accidentally touch and mute the open strings ringing below them.

    Practice Slow Transitions

    Switch back and forth between two chords—such as G Major and C Major—slowly and cleanly before speeding up your strumming hand.
